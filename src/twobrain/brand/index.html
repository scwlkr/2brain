03 / Form & voice

Rounded edges. Clear intentions.

Leave space around the mark.

Keep at least one stroke-width of clear space on every side. Use the symbol at 24 px or larger; below that, use the name in text.

Keep the shape intact.

Use the supplied monochrome and reverse variants. Do not stretch, rotate, add outlines, or recolor the mark outside this palette.

Say the useful thing.

“Local context is ready.”

Short, specific, and human. Use a system monospace in the terminal and a rounded system sans elsewhere. The path-drawn wordmark is an approximation of the board, not an identified font.
